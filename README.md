## Why I built this

Vertical B2B SaaS grows fastest when it lines up with platforms and organizations its buyers already rely on. I built the playbook and ran the motion for both a deep strategic product partnership and a portfolio of annual marketing partnerships. Two different deal architectures, two different sets of mechanics, one attribution layer connecting both.

## What I owned

- Partnership sourcing and evaluation across both partnership types
- Playbook build for the strategic product partnership motion
- Joint launch and ongoing programming across both partnership types
- Lead routing coordination between our AEs and the partner's AEs and CS teams
- Live landing page as internal source of truth for the strategic partnership
- Tracking link setup and attribution for annual marketing partnerships
- Partner-attributed pipeline reporting inside HubSpot

## Type 1: Strategic product partnership

The deep one. Playbook I built covered every surface of the deal:

- **Product integration.** API built into the product for a two-way workflow between the two platforms. Coordinated with Product and Eng on scoping and rollout.
- **Cross-team lead flow.** Partner's AEs and CS teams took responsibility for sourcing leads into our funnel. Our AEs worked those leads through our sales motion. Lead routing coordinated inside HubSpot with a dedicated partner-sourced routing rule.
- **Co-marketing.** Joint booth presence at industry events.
- **Partnership webinars.** Recurring cadence, joint audience, joint promotion.
- **Email marketing.** Coordinated blasts to both customer bases at launch moments and ongoing.
- **Shared enablement infrastructure.** Live landing page acting as an internal source of truth for both companies' AE and CS teams. Every resource, current campaign, current message, one place, always up to date. Killed the version-control problem that breaks most cross-company sales motions.

The playbook made the motion repeatable. Nothing depended on me sitting in a meeting to make it work.

## Type 2: Annual marketing partnerships

A portfolio of partnerships with ICP-fit organizations. Marketing-only, no product integration. Each partnership contract included a defined set of surfaces:

- Joint webinars on a shared cadence
- Email blasts to their audience at key launch moments
- Podcast collaboration (guest appearances, cross-promotion)
- Booth presence at their annual events
- Blog citations and backlinks into our content (compounds SEO and AEO authority over time)
- Newsletter mentions in their existing distribution

Every partnership had tracking links attached so pipeline could be attributed accurately per organization. Portfolio-level reporting rolled up to show which partnerships were producing and which needed renegotiation at renewal.

## Results

- $7M+ contributed pipeline from the strategic product partnership
- Majority of annual pipeline sourced from partnership motion overall
- Annual marketing partnership portfolio produced attributed pipeline and long-tail SEO and AEO authority signals
- Live enablement infrastructure kept both companies' sales conversations aligned in real time

## Principles

- One deep product partnership plus a portfolio of marketing partnerships is stronger than either alone
- Product integrations only make sense when both companies have real workflow overlap
- Partnership motion needs its own lead routing rule; without it, partner-sourced deals get worked inconsistently
- Live enablement infrastructure beats one-time collateral for cross-company sales alignment
- Every marketing partnership needs its own tracking layer or it's not really a partnership
- Blog citations and backlinks from partnership content compound over time for SEO and AEO
